Tasks for Windows

Install Tasks-Setup-v0.86.0.exe on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Updates retain your
existing tasks, theme, pairing and taskbar identity. The portable Tasks-PC.exe
remains available. Microsoft WebView2 is installed if needed.

You can use Tasks immediately, offline, without an account. Windows now has the
same Today/Upcoming views, task creation and full editor, projects, priorities,
labels, notes, checklists, repeat schedules, rescheduling, trash, completion,
in-progress actions, ordering and theme settings as the phone app.

Connect > Google sync > Sign in with Google. Sign into the same Google account on
your Android Tasks 0.86.0 app under Settings > Google sync. Tasks and theme merge
through Google Drive's private app-data folder; your other Drive files are not
accessed. The Google project is in testing, so use an account added as a test user.
Your browser handles Google sign-in. No Google password is entered into Tasks.

Windows protects its refresh token with the current Windows user's DPAPI key.
Android asks Google Play services for short-lived access tokens and does not store
a refresh token. Sign out keeps the local task list. Changes save offline and merge
when a connection returns. Use Sync now to request an immediate sync.

Local QR pairing remains available under Settings > Local pairing. Keep both apps
open on the same Wi-Fi for LAN sync. Google sync runs while Tasks is open on the
phone and while its PC background process is running. The Android widget and
Android reminder/notification controls remain phone features.

Closing the PC window keeps sync running. Reopen from the same shortcut. Quit the
PC companion from its pairing window to stop it. Task data lives separately from
the install in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tasks and survives upgrades and uninstall.

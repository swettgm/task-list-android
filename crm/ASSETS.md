# Icon sources

The Snapchat ghost silhouette is from [Simple Icons v16.0.0](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.0.0/icons/snapchat.svg), distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.0.0/LICENSE.md). Only the SVG path is embedded; the app sets its yellow color. Snapchat is a trademark of Snap Inc.

The bold single-person launcher outline is drawn for this app. Its background is transparent. The Discord mask and In person silhouette are local SVG drawings.

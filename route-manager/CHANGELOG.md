# Changelog

## 1.0.9

- Choose entries for notes and stop-list exports using checkboxes and Select all.
- Selected entries always number consecutively from 1, including across route headings.
- Selections stay local to the open export dialog and never change saved notes or stops.

## 1.0.8

- Left-aligned all export text, including note lines, in preview, Copy and Share.

## 1.0.7

- Export each day’s notes from the header with Copy or Share.
- Clean numbered lists include stop names, saved account numbers and complete note text. Shared notes appear under each selected stop; general notes remain included.

## 1.0.6

- Inverted the launcher icon to navy artwork on a white background. The in-app theme is unchanged.

## 1.0.5

- Persistent 1-day/2-day-ahead checkboxes shift the weekday route selected on launch/foreground. Friday wraps to Monday. Turning both off restores the current weekday route.

- Optional account numbers distinguish same-named stops, including autocomplete, note labels, stop editing and deletion. Existing stop IDs and note links are preserved.
- Export a clean numbered list of stops, account numbers and frequencies for all routes or one route. Copy to clipboard or open Android sharing to choose a messaging app.

## 1.0.4

- Route tabs with saved notes get a subtle highlight and dot; the active route keeps its white selection. Indicators refresh after note saves and deletion.
- Stop saves capitalize the first letter of every word, including from the disk button, automatic save with a note, and Add/Edit stop. Existing internal capitalization is preserved.

## 1.0.3

- Manage stops now includes all five routes grouped by route and weekday. Edit any route from Settings, or choose the route when adding a stop.

- Replaced the per-route stop/note count with a total count of notes across all five route pages, shown on every page.
- The total updates when notes are added or deleted; a note assigned to multiple stops counts once.

## 1.0.2

- Added Monthly alongside Weekly and Biweekly for saved stops. All notes remain visible.
- Manage stops and stop editors identify the route and weekday. Same-named stops on different routes keep separate frequencies and notes.

## 1.0.1

- Compact Add note editor with a one-line note field that grows with text, intact field labels and safe keyboard-height scrolling.
- Matched the Tasks settings icon, removed the main stop filter/Stops button and moved stop management into Settings.

## 1.0.0

- Separate Route Manager app with five looping route pages and automatic weekday selection.
- Saved-stop autocomplete, disk save button and notes associated with one or more stops.
- Weekly/Biweekly labels without hiding notes.
- Note and stop editing, confirmed deletion, clickable web links and appearance settings.
